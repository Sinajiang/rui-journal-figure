# Prompt — prototype → promote / repair / rollback

The architecture optimizer has produced one or more candidate figure sets.

## Step 1 — build structural prototypes
Use the actual candidate architecture and panel inventory.

The prototype must show:
- figure count;
- panel count;
- role;
- chart type;
- claim mapping;
- relative density.

Do not display fabricated data.

Mark:
`STRUCTURAL PROTOTYPE — NO DATA`

## Step 2 — compare to accepted baseline
Evaluate:
- anchor visibility;
- panel balance;
- density;
- whitespace;
- schematic fraction;
- journal fit;
- architecture score.

Compare against the accepted baseline, not only the most recent failed attempt.

## Step 3 — decision
Choose:

### PROMOTE
When the candidate is scientifically valid, visually passes, and does not materially regress.

### REPAIR
When science/architecture is sound but local visual defects remain.

### ROLLBACK
When global visual quality, scientific coverage, or journal fit becomes meaningfully worse.

### HOLD
When QA or manual review is incomplete.

## Step 4 — surgical repair
If REPAIR:
- localize defect;
- protect accepted baseline features;
- make the smallest change;
- re-render;
- re-run QA;
- compare again to baseline.

## Step 5 — final-data rendering
Only after architecture PROMOTE:
- render real data;
- verify scientific sentinels;
- run geometry/journal/evidence QA;
- perform final-size manual inspection.

A prototype promotion is not equivalent to final figure approval.
