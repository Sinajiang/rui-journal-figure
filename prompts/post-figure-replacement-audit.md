# Prompt — post-figure-replacement audit

A new figure version has been promoted.

Do not assume the manuscript still matches.

Check:
1. every Results citation to the figure;
2. every panel letter;
3. the legend architecture;
4. supplementary numbering;
5. source-data references;
6. claim strength and evidence status.

Generate only the minimum manuscript edits required to restore exact alignment.
Do not alter scientific results merely to match a redesigned figure.
