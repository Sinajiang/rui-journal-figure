# Prompt — figure architecture optimization

Use the completed:

- Methods / Results extraction;
- source-table inventory;
- evidence graph;
- benchmark dossier;
- journal contract;
- panel candidate inventory.

Do not add a panel that requires unavailable evidence.

## Step 1 — candidate panel inventory
For each possible panel define:
- claim(s);
- source(s);
- role;
- evidence family;
- chart type;
- availability;
- priority;
- contribution value;
- benchmark support;
- traceability;
- visual density;
- narrative order.

## Step 2 — redundancy audit
Identify candidate pairs that may repeat:
- the same claim;
- the same source;
- the same evidence family;
- the same chart grammar.

Do not delete automatically. Decide whether the second panel adds inferential value.

## Step 3 — architecture search
Generate 3-, 4-, and 5-main-figure architectures when permitted by journal/project constraints.

For each architecture:
- preserve must-main evidence;
- maximize weighted claim coverage;
- maximize contribution visibility;
- diversify evidence roles;
- control panel density;
- move repetitive material to Supplementary;
- keep benchmark figure-count patterns as soft priors only.

## Step 4 — panel actions
Recommend:
- PROMOTE
- KEEP
- REPAIR
- MERGE
- DEMOTE
- SPLIT_FIGURE
- UPGRADE_CANDIDATE
- DROP_NOT_APPLICABLE

## Step 5 — expert promotion
Select the architecture that best balances:
scientific fidelity + claim coverage + contribution visibility + benchmark grammar + journal fit + visual feasibility.

The top numerical score is advisory, not self-authorizing.
