# Prompt — evidence-density and contribution optimization

After architecture selection but before final prototype approval:

1. score every candidate panel for scientific contribution;
2. estimate visual density;
3. identify the anchor result in each figure;
4. allocate visual area in proportion to contribution and density;
5. cap workflow/provenance area unless design itself is a major contribution;
6. audit contribution-area inversions;
7. identify panels for PROMOTE / KEEP / COMPACT / MERGE / DEMOTE;
8. render a weighted no-data prototype;
9. compare with the accepted baseline and benchmark/domain grammar.

Do not use P value alone as visual-priority score.
Do not shrink text to compensate for an overfull architecture.
Do not demote must-main evidence for cosmetic simplicity.
